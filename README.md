# HR Analysis Dashboard (Power BI)

## Project Overview
This project presents an interactive HR Analytics Dashboard built using Microsoft Power BI to analyze workforce demographics, employee attrition, job satisfaction, and organizational trends.

The dashboard helps HR teams and management make data-driven decisions by identifying attrition patterns and employee engagement factors.
---

## Business Problem
Employee attrition can significantly impact organizational productivity and hiring costs. This dashboard provides insights into:
* Employee demographics
* Attrition trends
* Department-wise employee turnover
* Job satisfaction levels
* Education background analysis
* Workforce distribution
---

## Dashboard Features

### KPI Metrics
* Total Employees
* Active Employees
* Total Attrition
* Attrition Rate
* Average Age
* Average Monthly Pay

### Employee Demographics
* Age Group Distribution
* Gender Distribution
* Marital Status Analysis

### Attrition Analysis
* Attrition by Department
* Attrition by Age
* Attrition by Education Field
* Travel Frequency Impact

### Job Satisfaction Analysis
* Job Satisfaction Ratings
* Satisfaction by Job Role

## Key Insights
* Total Employees: 588
* Active Employees: 501
* Attrition Rate: 14.8%
* Highest Attrition observed in R&D and Sales departments.
* Sales Representatives show the highest attrition rate among job roles.
* Workforce is primarily concentrated in the 25–44 age group.
